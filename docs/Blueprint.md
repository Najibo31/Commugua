# **App Name**: Commugua Project

## Core Features:

- Home Page: Display a home page introducing the mosque development project, highlighting its purpose and vision.
- History Section: Showcase the history of the Muslim community in Guadeloupe (Commugua).
- Prayer Times: Dynamically display prayer times fetched from an API, overlaid on a relevant background image.
- Islam Pillars: Explain the core principles and five pillars of Islam with clear, concise descriptions.
- Donations Information: Provide information for in person donations, specifying donations are pledge-based and hand-to-hand.
- News Section: Display a scrolling list of the mosque's latest news and updates.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft green (#90EE90) to represent growth and tranquility, aligning with the project's nurturing essence.
- Background color: Very light desaturated green (#F0FFF0), providing a serene backdrop that enhances readability and visual appeal.
- Accent color: A slightly more vibrant, analogous green (#7CFC00) to draw attention to calls to action.
- A logo in the header
- Use clear and readable typography to ensure content is accessible.
- Employ subtle and meaningful icons to complement the text and enhance user experience.
- Design a responsive layout that adapts seamlessly to various devices, ensuring optimal viewing on desktops, tablets, and smartphones.
- Incorporate subtle scrolling effects in the news section to capture and maintain user attention.
